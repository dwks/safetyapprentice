Why this website

I am a Senior Research Manager at ERA. I see many thousands of applications from people who want to get into AI safety. ERA cannot scale to this level; no fellowship can.

So this site is an attempt to put a lot of useful resources in one place, and let people self-learn -- the guidance that would otherwise only reach the small number of people who make it through a program.

One extra step

A paid career call

Beyond the free resources here, there's one extra step: a paid career call with me, and eventually with other people too.

There are free mentoring services out there, and they're worth using — but demand exceeds supply. Paid calls are a way to make more of that time available.

dwkmentoring.com →

I'm also hoping this turns out to be a reasonable way to supplement AI safety salaries, and that others may want to use the same model in future.
