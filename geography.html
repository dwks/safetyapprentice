Geography of
AI Safety

AI safety work doesn't happen in a vacuum. These are the coworking spaces, research hubs, and cities where alignment researchers cluster — and where you can find collaborators.

Why location matters

Being physically near other safety researchers dramatically accelerates your work. Hallway conversations, whiteboard sessions, and spontaneous collaborations are how many of the best ideas in alignment have started. If you can, consider relocating to one of these hubs — even temporarily.

Not near a hub? Start online.

Many of these communities have active online presences. Join the AI Alignment Forum, EleutherAI Discord, or the AI Safety Fundamentals course to start connecting with researchers worldwide. When you're ready, visiting a hub for even a few weeks can be transformative.
